# neue-dot-com™

An independent open-source collective based in Malaysia.

---

## The Concept
neue-dot-com™ is a collaborative workspace designed to build practical software tools and cultivate a localized open-source ecosystem. The collective operates as an open digital framework, allowing contributors to develop accessible technology without corporate barriers.

---

## Organization Structure
The governance and maintenance of neue-dot-com™ are split into three core disciplines to ensure long-term stability:

* **Technology:** Designing and maintaining public repositories, lightweight utilities, and developer APIs.
* **Legal Operations:** Establishing governance policies, managing administrative compliance, and protecting organizational assets.
* **Finance:** Oversight of logistical resources, budget management, and operational accounting.
